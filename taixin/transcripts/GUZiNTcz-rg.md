Last year, our designer Yunpin, he told me during a meeting, 'Boss, wait a second, I have something to tell you.' I said, 'What is it? Say it.' He said, 'Boss, I want to go abroad for two and a half months.' Eh? Sounds a bit weird, right? You've been with the company for less than a year, and you want to go abroad for two and a half months. Suddenly I thought of something. When I was a salesperson before, I didn't dare to ask my supervisor for leave at all. So I felt envious in my heart and said, 'Fortunately, I'm your boss!'
