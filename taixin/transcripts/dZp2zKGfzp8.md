Can testimonial examples be fully trusted? Experience cannot be duplicated. The reason is that the human brain has a mechanism that easily falls into the placebo effect and the issue of brain activity. When I was a child, we lived in the countryside of Miaoli. Every week, my grandmother would take me to the temple. A small truck would drive in, and they would start performing. They would start talking about who took this 'universal medicine' and got cured. Cancer was cured, diabetes was cured, high blood pressure was cured, and even asthma was cured. So my grandmother bought several bottles of medicine. It wasn't until I got home that I realized I had taken it for two weeks and nothing had changed, and the medicine was very expensive. Why do I care so much that testimonial cases cannot be used as a basis for argument? The reason lies in the placebo effect. Perhaps 70% of diseases in the human body will heal naturally. So as long as I make a miracle medicine for you and give it to 100 people, 70 people will get better after taking it. Then you will think my medicine is miraculous, but in fact, what I prescribed was just Vitamin C. This is the placebo effect. Therefore, the placebo effect cannot be used as a basis for argument. The second is the issue of brain activity. For example, if you are researching a car today, looking at a BMW for a long time, and after three months of research, you have analyzed all the data and fuel consumption. You decide to buy a BMW. As a result, the day before you buy it, you hear your friend say that he bought a BMW that was terrible, and the new car was repaired 30 times in three months. Then you most likely won't buy it. The brain is called a cognitive miser. A cognitive miser means it will use the least amount of energy to come up with a conclusion. The less energy the brain consumes, the longer you will survive in primitive society. We will only look at the most obvious case.
